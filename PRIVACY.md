# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 31, 2026_

168 doesn't collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your data. That's the whole policy — here's the detail for anyone who wants it.

## What we collect

Nothing. 168 has no analytics, no ads, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests at all. There's no account to create and nothing to sign into.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter — categories, weekly hour budgets, timer sessions — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself (Setup → Export), and even then it's saved to a file you choose. We never see it.

## Live Activities & widgets

168 uses Apple's ActivityKit and WidgetKit to show a running timer on your Lock Screen. That's handled entirely by iOS on your device — it isn't sent to us or anyone else.

## Children's privacy

Since we don't collect anything from anyone, we don't collect anything from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
